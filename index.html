PLAYGROUND

50 games, tools, and toys that run straight in the browser. Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript: no frameworks, no build step, nothing to install.

Every thumbnail below is drawn live by the page. Hover one and it moves. The source is on GitHub and pull requests are welcome.

Highlights

  • Vice CityOpen-world driving in a procedural city with traffic, police chases, and delivery jobs. Street-level 3D camera drawn on a plain 2D canvas.
  • BreachFirst-person shooter on a raycasting engine written from scratch. Every wall texture, sprite, and sound is generated in code.
  • Road RashMotorcycle combat racing on a pseudo-3D road: punch and kick rivals, steal their clubs, dodge two-way traffic, buy faster bikes.
  • Arrow FluxSlide tangled arrows off a packed board. The generator builds every board in solution order, so each one is provably solvable.
