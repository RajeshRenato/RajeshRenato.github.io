PLAYGROUND

104 games, tools, and toys that run straight in your browser. Merge PDFs, send files, or make a video call without uploading anything, and play games with friends over a link.

Free, no sign-up, nothing to install. It's all plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and every thumbnail is drawn live by the page. The source is on GitHub and pull requests are welcome.
