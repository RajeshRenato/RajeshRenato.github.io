Flag Dash

A 60 second flag quiz. You see a flag and pick the country from four names. Name as many as you can before time runs out.

Rules

  • A right answer is worth 1 point. Once you have 3 or more right in a row, each one is worth 2.
  • A wrong answer resets your streak and shows the correct country.
  • The flags are emoji, drawn from a list of 90 countries.
  • Your best score is saved in localStorage.
